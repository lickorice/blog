# I'm back, hopefully!

Evidently, I haven't really posted anything for around half a year now. Just got caught up with life and couldn't really find time to update my site—simple as that. Even these posts take me around 30 minutes to an hour (maybe more if I get sidetracked with some rabbit-holing) to write.

However, having these #synaptic-saturday posts sequenced like they are, they wander around in my mind; which I think is a good thing. "I've only posted four... I really have to post more, that *should* have been weekly!" So, without further ado, here's number five.
# Portfolio site update

I've updated my main portfolio site at https://carlospanganiban.com/ to more closely match what I feel who I am now, since the version prior to this was also made around six years ago, in COVID. I find this new version "more 'me'" and I'm pretty happy with it.

You'll see that I've done away with promoting my YouTube stuff since I don't really post there anymore (and honestly, kids these days could just go ask an LLM and get their personal tutor), and made it a hybrid resume-personal site thing. More of it, in fact taking the spotlight, are personal details about me rather than who I worked for or what I did. In fact, the professional details now take up the **bottom 30% of the page** now, as compared to putting it up top.

Why? It's a terrible way to promote yourself professionally, right? Well, I think we've all been too caught up [[min-maxing]] our lives (or what I've heard other friends say, living too much of a "Western life") that we forget the point of it all. I pretty much realized this when I **got off social media** for good and saw that my site was the only way other people can reach me directly.
# Getting off Meta

And on that note, yes, I'm off Meta for good. In the Philippines, most if not all people are on the platforms—Facebook and Instagram; which heightens the friction of one *leaving* said sites. Fortunately, I've slowly coerced my closest friends to go on dedicated messaging platforms like Telegram or Discord. Worst case, I could still message people on LinkedIn or Steam.

Why did I go?

...is probably the wrong question to ask. Why shouldn't *you* go? It takes up too much time, and for me, only ~20% of it are updates from friends I don't even keep in touch with that much. "Oh, that guy from X years ago did Y!" Not to be jaded, I'd still have a drink or a smoke with *guy from X* if I see them (and if I get cordially invited to do so), but sacrificing hours of my life being updated with events I'm not really involved in I think is an extremely unhealthy lifestyle.

The remaining eighty percent *used to be* entertaining. Man, I even used to be on TikTok for the memes. But I think Meta's revenue is sucking so badly that I'm getting incessantly peddled with adverts. Or "content creators" making "content" that are ads in disguise. Is that why they started calling themselves "content creators" rather than "entertainers" now? Is "content" a euphemism for political and/or corporate propaganda?

Excuse me for my tirade—I really am done with contemporary social media. I find that there's basically zero impact on my social life as my social life already lives outside of it anyway. I talk to my friends on Telegram/Discord, play with them on Steam, enjoy their company *in the flesh*.
## What I'm still on

But I'm not fully a "technohermit" in this "[technofeudalist](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technofeudalism)" world. I'm still on Reddit and YouTube, where I fully enjoy my time. Yes, there are ads on both platforms but they're not as intrusive (or my adblock is just that good). I even have [SponsorBlock](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mnjggcdmjocbbbhaepdhchncahnbgone?utm_source=item-share-cb) to automatically skip the endorsements on my YouTube vids.

I'm also active on some forums online, and you can find updates about me here on this site.
